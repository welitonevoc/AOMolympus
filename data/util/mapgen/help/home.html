Welcome to Help!

 

If you are looking for help with one of MapGen's features, click the appropriate link in the Reference section in the sidebar. If you are new to MapGen, the best way to get started is the Tutorial - it will guide you through creating your first map and show you how to use most of the features in the program. For some general advice, check Tips and Tricks. If you have questions that aren't answered here, you might be able to find some help at the Woad Creations forums.

Note: MapGen Help is best viewed using Internet Explorer version 6.0 or higher.
