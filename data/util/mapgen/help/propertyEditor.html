The Class Properties Editor

 

You can choose global settings for your map in the panel below the map filename. The following settings are available:

Map Name - The name your map will have on the list of random maps in Age of Mythology.

Map Icon - The image displayed for your map in the list of maps in AoM. You can choose the image of one of the standard maps, or a god or civilization icon, as well as several other icons. If you want to use a texture that is not listed in this dropdown, just type it in. You can use custom-made 256x256 TGA or DDT image files from the Age of Mythology\texture folder for map icons, but be aware that these will not transfer over multiplayer with your map. You can also use any texture in Age of Mythology, if you know its name, though textures that are not 256x256 may not look as good in the list.

Starting Units - Choose what the players will each start with. The following choices are available:

  • Units and Transport - This is the recommended choice for multiplayer games. It is similar to the "Nomad" random map type. Players will start with some villagers/dwarves as well as 3 Ulfsarks, Ox Cart, Pharaoh, Priest and/or Hyena of Set depending on their major god choice, and also with enough resources to build a first Town Center on a Settlement. If players are surrounded by water, they will start on an island with a Transport as well as the above units. There will be a 3-minute Ceasefire during which build rates are sped up but players cannot cast god powers or attack each other, to give everyone a chance to get settled. Then the game will run as usual. Note that computer players may not know how to play in this mode, unless you are using a specially made AI script.
  • Town Center Only - Players get a Town Center and the starting units for their civilization. They will have to build up and look for resources like in a standard Random Map game. This mode is useful if you want to play against computer players.
  • Town Center and Towers - Players get 4 Sentry Towers as well as a Town Center and their starting units.

Default Terrain - The type of terrain placed on the map before it is painted with your grid square classes, or "Water" to start the map with water. In general, you should not leave any space on the grid layout black, and the default terrain should not matter.

Default Water Type - The type of water the map is based on. This will affect how shorelines are painted. It should be the same as the main kind of waterbody you use on your map.

World Lighting Set - The lighting set (sun position, sun color, sun brightness, etc) used for your map. In general, Default is the best choice, but sometimes a slightly moodier set like Alfheim or Erebus can be useful. You can also type in the name of a custom lighting set, but these don't transfer over multiplayer along with your map.

Tiles Per Player (Normal) - Determines the map size for Normal sized maps. Note that Gaia, the "world player", is counted as a player here, to make sure there is extra space on smaller maps. The map will contain TilesPerPlayer x (NumberOfPlayers + 1) x RandomMultiplier tiles, where RandomMultiplier is between 95% and 105% to add variance. It is recommended that you use around 9,000 tiles per player on most maps, but you might need more on water maps with little land area to build on.

Tiles Per Player (Large) - Same as above, for the Large map size.

Day/Night Cycle Time - MapGen can add triggers to your map to change lighting for day and night. Type in the time, in seconds, of the day-night cycle animation you want, or "0" (zero) for no day/night cycle. A good choice is anywhere between 240 and 420 (4 to 7 minutes). The cycle time is split up as follows: 45% day,  transition, 11% dusk, 33% night, 11% dawn, repeat. The lighting fades smoothly between these different periods.
