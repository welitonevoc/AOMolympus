About MapGen

 

MapGen is an Age of Mythology real-world map generating program by Matei, a member in Woad Creations. Version 1.0 was released publicly in August, 2003.

The MapGen logo is a modified version of the the icon for the "Implode" god power in Age of Mythology: The Titans™, a game by Ensemble Studios / Microsoft.

MapGen was designed to work with Age of Mythology version 1.06, though theoretically it should function well with later patches and with the expansion pack.

Visit the Downloads section of Age of Mythology Heaven for further updates to this program.

Age of Mythology™ is a game by Ensemble Studios / Microsoft.
